Category

PAN Card

How do I apply for a new PAN card?

Select the PAN Card service from our Services page, upload the required documents, complete the payment, and our team will process your application.

Which documents are required for a PAN card application?

You typically need an identity proof, an address proof and a recent passport-size photograph. Exact requirements are listed on the service details page.

Can I correct my name or date of birth on an existing PAN card?

Yes, our PAN Correction service allows you to update your name, date of birth or photograph on an existing PAN card.

How long does PAN card processing usually take?

Processing generally takes 7 to 10 business days, though this may vary based on government processing timelines.

Category

Aadhaar Services

Can I update my Aadhaar address through Your Cuber?

Yes, we assist with Aadhaar address updates by helping you prepare and submit the required documents correctly.

How long does an Aadhaar address update take?

Processing time varies but is generally completed within 3 to 5 business days after submission.

Can I update my mobile number linked to Aadhaar?

Mobile number updates require an in-person visit to an official Aadhaar centre, and our team can guide you on the correct process to follow.

Is my Aadhaar information kept secure with Your Cuber?

Yes, all Aadhaar-related documents and information are handled with strict confidentiality throughout the process.

Category

Certificates

Which certificates can I apply for through Your Cuber?

We assist with Income Certificates, Caste Certificates, Residence Certificates and other commonly required government certificates.

What documents are needed for an income certificate?

You will generally need an identity proof, address proof and relevant income related documents. Full requirements are listed on the service details page.

Can I apply for a certificate on behalf of a family member?

Yes, you can apply on behalf of a family member as long as you provide their required documents and consent where applicable.

How will I receive my completed certificate?

Completed certificates are shared digitally through your account or WhatsApp, with physical collection available if required.

Voter ID

Can I register as a new voter through Your Cuber?

Yes, we assist first-time applicants with new voter ID registration, including document preparation and form submission.

Can I correct details on my existing voter ID?

Yes, our Voter ID Assistance service also covers corrections to name, address and other details on existing voter ID cards.

What documents are required for voter ID registration?

You generally need an age proof, address proof and a recent photograph. Full requirements are listed on the service details page.

How long does voter ID processing take?

Voter ID processing typically takes 10 to 15 business days depending on government verification timelines.

Job Form Fill-up

Which job forms can Your Cuber help me fill?

We assist with a wide range of government and private job application forms, including railway, SSC and other recruitment applications.

What if I miss the application deadline?

We recommend submitting your details well before the deadline. Our team will always inform you of upcoming deadlines during the process.

Will you verify my details before submission?

Yes, our team carefully reviews all entered details against your documents before final submission to minimize errors.

Can I get a copy of my submitted application form?

Yes, a copy of your submitted application and confirmation receipt is shared with you once the form has been filed.

Category

Payments

Is online payment on Your Cuber secure?

Yes, all payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways to protect your financial information.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking through our secure payment partners.

What happens if my payment fails but money is deducted?

If an amount is deducted without a successful order confirmation, it is automatically refunded by the payment provider within a few business days. Please contact support if you don't see the refund reflected.

Will I receive a payment receipt?

Yes, a digital payment receipt is generated automatically and made available in your account after every successful transaction.

Document Upload

Can I upload documents from my mobile phone?

Yes, you can capture and upload documents directly from your phone's camera or gallery through our online portal.

What file formats are accepted for document upload?

We accept common formats such as JPG, PNG and PDF for uploaded documents.

Is there a file size limit for uploads?

Yes, each uploaded file should generally be under a few megabytes. If your file is too large, our support team can guide you on compressing it.

What if I uploaded the wrong document by mistake?

Please contact our support team as soon as possible so we can help you replace the document before your application is processed.
