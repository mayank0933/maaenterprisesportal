Need Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I track my request online?

Yes, you can track your order anytime using this page by entering your Order ID and registered mobile number.

Where do I find my Order ID?

Your Order ID is shared with you immediately after submitting your application, both on-screen and via WhatsApp or email.

What if my order status shows no results?

Please double-check your Order ID and mobile number for typos. If the issue continues, reach out to our support team for help.

How often is the order status updated?

Your order status is updated at every key stage of processing, from verification through to final delivery.
